## Role Distribuiton

The initial role distribution will be William as the assignment leader and meeting leader, Sultan as the meeting recorder, and Mateo as the project monitor. We feel that a role rotation after every major deadline is the most fair way to ensure that everyone is equally fulfilling each role. It's important to note that we don't plan on having a specific rotation order, rather selecting what each member would like to do for that deadline, as long as it is different then what they did previously. It is also important to note that we have only three members rather than the typical group of four. This means that each deadline, someone will be fulfilling two roles rather than just a singular one, most likely this will be in the form of the assignment leader being combined with something else. We chose this role to combine as it has the least demanding workload attached directly to it. As there is the possibility of one member having a more demanding work load one week to another, if needed we can share or split the responsibility as needed. Milestone tracking and meetings will all be tracked through a share Google Calendar. Technical responsibilities will be assigned as evenly as possible, the end goal is to learn from this class, we don't want a singular individual taking control of one aspect and the other members loosing out on the opportunity to learn in those aspects. 

## Team Coordination & Accountability 
As we are communicating primarily through Discord, it is extremely easy to do have rapid back and forth communication, it should be easy to communicate deadline responsibilities and ensure that everyone is satisfied with the state of the assignment before it is submitted. 

The main reason we want to have at least one in person meeting during the week is to be able to accurately gauge the proficiency of a specific member at their assigned task. If they don't know how to complete something, this is the time to get assistance from another member that potentially has the required skill set, or to realize that they need to attend an office hour to get extra assistance. 

Any design feedback will be shared in the discord via either a screenshot of the comments or copy and pasting the feedback. 

Missed contributions or assignment responsibilities will be handled on a case by case bases, and depending on the level of communication to the rest of the team will be handled accordingly. 

If a team member doesn't act in accordance with this charter, depending on the reason behind the charter violation, either a revaluation and redistribution of tasks moving forward, or moving to the conflict resolution section, looking at contacting an instructor for further guidance. 

In the event that a member is underperforming, it will be up to the other members to discuss a solution, whether that be reducing the workload or providing extra assistance week to week. 

In the event that the member does not improve, it will be important to note why they are not improving, and to first revaluate either workload or assistance. Only after these steps have been taken would we look towards a pink slip or any sort of more drastic action. 

## Conflict Recognition & Resolution

When conflict inevitably arrives in the team, whether it be a basic disagreement of ideas, or not being to find common ground on how to move forward with the project. The most important way to resolve these issues is to talk and make sure that everyone understand the position of each member in disagreement. If at this point, talking through each members standing doesn't resolve the conflict, either bringing in a neutral third party or taking a break from the issue and moving onto a different problem for the time being and coming back at a later date. 

The only time to escalate a conflict to the professor is if a team member either disappears, or completely falls out of loop with the project.
