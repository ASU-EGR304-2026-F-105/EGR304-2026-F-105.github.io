## Project Objective

This project aims to develop a portable and reliable off grid communication device for users traveling or working in areas where cellular and internet service is limited or unavailable. The product will provide users with a dependable method of sending and receiving messages, sharing location information, and communicating during emergency situations. The device will feature a built in screen with a compact physical keyboard so that it can maintain its essential functionality, even without a physical connection to another device. The product will focus on improving several weaknesses identified through research of existing off grid communication devices. The device will provide reliable messaging in remote environments, long battery life for multi day use, clear message delivery confirmation, and an intuitive interface with minimal menu navigation. The physical design will emphasize portability, durability, and controls that are easy to operate in outdoor conditions. By combining standalone communication with computer connectivity, the product aims to provide users with a simple and dependable communication system that remains functional when conventional communication networks or personal smartphones are not available. 

## Stakeholders

* **Target group:** Hikers, campers, hunters, backpackers, remote workers, emergency preparedness users, and others traveling outside or who might get cut off from the cellular network.
* **Target purchaser:** Outdoor enthusiast, travelers, workers in remote locations, individuals and others who travel or work in locations where cellular communication may be unavailable, friends/family of above mentioned outdoor enthusiasts, individuals who live in disaster prone locations, where cellular may go down.
* **Customer service:** Provides technical support for device setup, connectivity, messaging, computer integration, account issues, and troubleshooting. Customer service should have clear diagnostic information available to help users resolve communication problems quickly, especially before remote trips. 
* **Marketing & Sales division:** Focuses on communicating the product's key advantages, including reliable off grid communication, standalone operation without a smartphone, long battery life, built in screen and keyboard, location sharing, and emergency capabilities. 
* **Retailers:** Prefer a durable, easily packaged product that can be safely transported and stored while providing clear information about its capabilities and intended applications. 

## Use Cases

### User Story #1: Maya

Maya is a 29-year-old hiker who regularly takes weekend trips into remote desert and mountain areas where cell service is unreliable or completely unavailable. She enjoys hiking alone because it gives her a sense of independence, but she also wants confidence that she can reach help if something goes wrong. For her, the most important need is dependable emergency communication beyond the reach of cellular networks.

Before each trip, Maya sends her route to her family and uses her device to share simple check-in messages along the way. She does not want to spend time navigating complicated menus while tired or stressed, so quick access to preset messages and clear delivery confirmation are important. A long-lasting battery also matters because she needs the device to remain powered throughout the trip, not just during the first few hours.

### User Story #2 : David

David is a 46-year-old volunteer coordinator for a neighborhood emergency preparedness group. His team prepares for events such as wildfires, power outages, and severe storms, where cellular networks may become overloaded or unavailable. During these situations, his main concern is that critical messages reach the right people reliably and on time.

David’s group includes people with very different levels of technical experience, so the device needs to be simple enough for members to use under stress. He cares most about dependable message delivery, clear confirmation that messages were sent, and communication features that still work without relying on a smartphone. The device gives the group confidence that they can coordinate basic safety actions even when normal communication systems fail.

## Aspects

The new product will be a portable off-grid communication device designed around the user needs identified through research of existing satellite communicators and two-way radios. The P1–P10 designation indicates the priority of each requirement, from low to high.

1. **Producer Design**
      * 1.1 The product shall include a built-in screen capable of clearly displaying messages, device status, battery level, and connection status. (P10)
      * 1.2 The product shall include a compact physical keyboard that allows users to compose messages without a smartphone or computer. (P10)
      * 1.3 The product shall be compact and lightweight enough to comfortably carry during extended outdoor activities. (P8)
      * 1.4 The product shall use buttons and controls that can be reliably operated in outdoor conditions. (P8)
      * 1.5 The product shall use a durable enclosure capable of withstanding normal outdoor use, transportation, and minor impacts. (P9)
      * 1.6 The product shall include a rechargeable battery capable of supporting multi-day use under normal operating conditions. (P10)
      * 1.7 The product shall include a USB-C connection for charging and computer connectivity. (P8)
  
2. **Software / Functionality**
      * 2.1 The product shall send and receive messages in areas where cellular communication is unavailable. (P10)
      * 2.2 The product shall provide essential messaging functions without requiring a connected smartphone. (P10)
      * 2.3 The product shall provide location information that can be shared with selected contacts. (P9)
      * 2.4 The product shall provide an emergency communication function that operates independently of a smartphone. (P10)
      * 2.5 The product shall display the status of outgoing messages as sending, sent, delivered, or failed. (P10)
      * 2.6 The product shall store sent and received messages for later review. (P7)
      * 2.7 The product shall support software and firmware updates through a connected computer. (P7)
      * 2.8 The product shall provide warnings when communication cannot be established. (P9)

3. **Interactivity & User Experience**
      * 3.1 The product shall provide a simple interface that minimizes the number of menu levels required to access commonly used functions. (P9)
      * 3.2 The product shall provide quick access to messaging, contacts, location sharing, and emergency communication. (P10)
      * 3.3 The product shall provide clear visual feedback when a message has or has not been successfully delivered. (P10)
      * 3.4 The product shall clearly display remaining battery life and communication status. (P9)
      * 3.5 The product shall be usable by a first-time user with minimal instruction or training. (P8)
      * 3.6 The keyboard and controls shall provide sufficient feedback to indicate that an input has been registered. (P7)
      * 3.7 Frequently used features shall require minimal user interaction to access. (P8)

4. **Customization**
      * 4.1 The product shall allow users to create and store preset messages. (P7)
      * 4.2 The product shall allow users to create and manage a contact list. (P8)
      * 4.3 The product shall allow users to configure emergency contact information. (P9)
      * 4.4 The product shall allow users to adjust display brightness and notification settings. (P5)      
      * 4.5 The product shall provide configurable power-saving options to extend battery life. (P7)
      * 4.6 The product shall allow contacts, preset messages, and device settings to be managed through a connected computer. (P7)

5. **Manufacturing**
      * 5.1 The product shall use standardized components and connectors where practical to reduce manufacturing and repair complexity. (P7)
      * 5.2 The enclosure shall protect internal electronics from environmental exposure expected during normal outdoor use. (P9)
      * 5.3 The product shall be designed to minimize unnecessary components and assembly complexity. (P6)
      * 5.4 Each manufactured device shall undergo functional testing of its communication system, screen, keyboard, battery, charging system, and computer connection. (P9)
      * 5.5 The product shall be designed to allow replacement or repair of major components where practical. (P5)

6. **Safety**
      * 6.1 The product shall clearly notify the user when an emergency message has been successfully transmitted or has failed to transmit. (P10)
      * 6.2 The emergency function shall be protected against accidental activation while remaining quickly accessible during an emergency. (P10)
      * 6.3 The product shall provide a low-battery warning before reaching a critical battery level. (P9)
      * 6.4 The battery system shall include protection against overcharging, overheating, short circuits, and other foreseeable electrical faults. (P10)
      * 6.5 The product shall clearly inform users when communication service is unavailable or unreliable. (P10)
      * 6.6 The product shall comply with applicable radio-frequency, battery, electronic-device, and communication regulations for its intended market. (P10)

## Requirement Criteria Specifications

* 1.1.1 – The screen shall remain readable in both indoor and outdoor lighting conditions.
* 1.2.1 – The keyboard shall allow a user to compose and send a message entirely without a smartphone.
* 1.3.1 – The complete device shall weigh no more than 300 g.
* 1.5.1 – The enclosure shall achieve a minimum target rating of IP67 for water and dust resistance.
* 1.6.1 – The battery shall provide a target of at least 72 hours of typical operation between charges.
* 1.7.1 – The device shall support charging and data transfer through USB-C.
* 2.5.1 – The interface shall visually distinguish between sending, sent, delivered, and failed messages.
* 3.2.1 – Emergency communication shall be accessible within three user actions or fewer from the main screen.
* 3.5.1 – A first-time user shall be able to compose and send a basic message without consulting a full user manual.
* 6.3.1 – The device shall provide a visible low-battery warning before the remaining charge reaches a critical operating level.

## Open Questions

* Which off-grid communication technology will the product use: satellite, radio, mesh networking, or a combination?
* What satellite network or communication infrastructure will provide the best balance between coverage, cost, and power consumption?
* What screen technology will provide adequate outdoor visibility while minimizing battery consumption?
* What is the ideal keyboard size and layout for portability while remaining usable with larger hands or gloves?
Should the device support Bluetooth smartphone connectivity in addition to standalone operation?
Should computer connectivity require dedicated software, or should configuration be accessible through a standard interface?
* What level of water, dust, impact, and temperature resistance is appropriate for the target users?
* What battery capacity is required to reliably achieve the multi-day battery-life target?
Can major components such as the battery, keyboard, and screen be designed for repair or replacement?
* What emergency communication and regulatory requirements will apply based on the final communication technology?
