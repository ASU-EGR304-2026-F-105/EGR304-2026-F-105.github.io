## Team Charter

From the information which can be reviewed in ["Appendix: Team Organization"](https://asu-egr304-2026-f-105.github.io/EGR304-2026-F-105.github.io/Appendix/App-Team-Org/), the team's charter formed to be:

> Our team is committed to working collaboratively, communicating openly, and holding one another accountable to develop a reliable emergency communication device that functions without cellular or internet infrastructure. We will respect each team member's ideas, complete assigned responsibilities on time, and make decisions based on sound engineering principles to create a high-quality, user-centered prototype.




## Product Mission Statement

Describe the distilled purpose, function, or reason for your product to exist. From this form a team mission statement. Google's mission statement is:

 > “Offer a disaster-proof alternative to modern cellular/internet communication”
