## Team Introduction


>Team 105 consists of four undergraduate engineering students at Arizona State University enrolled in EGR 304: William Buckner, Mateo Delgado, Sultan Haidar Ali, and Joseph Rivera. Together, we are developing RadioLink, a portable, off-grid communication device designed to provide dependable wireless messaging during emergencies or in areas without conventional cellular or internet services. The project emphasizes reliability, portability, and ease of use, with each team member responsible for developing an individual hardware subsystem that contributes to the overall functionality of the device. These subsystems will be integrated through communication connections and a shared common ground, while maintaining independent voltage regulation. This website documents the team's research, planning, engineering decisions, and development process. Readers can explore the Team Organization, User Needs and Benchmarking, Product Requirements, Design Ideation, Team Block Diagram, and Software Proposal sections to learn more about the project's objectives, technical design, and progress.
